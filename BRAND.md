# Brand & positioning — Elite Interview Prep Platform

> Product name TBD. Update hero copy when finalized.

## The one sentence that sells

Most interview prep gives you fragments. We give you **complete topic mastery** — understand it, apply it, go deep, prove it — before you move on.

---

## Elevator pitch

The interview prep platform that doesn't let you skip ahead until you actually understand the topic — with definitions, runnable examples, deep dives, real interview Q&A, and hands-on coding challenges.

---

## Category

**Structured technical interview mastery** — not another LeetCode clone, not another video course.

Position against:
- **LeetCode / HackerRank** — algorithms only, no concept depth
- **GreatFrontEnd / blog posts** — fragmented Q&A, no progression
- **Frontend Masters / Udemy** — video-heavy, hard to revisit specific topics
- **MDN / docs** — reference, not interview-focused

---

## Brand pillars (every feature must support these)

| Pillar | Question | Product expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Understand** | Do I know what this actually is? | Clear definitions + mental models |
| **Apply** | Can I use it in code? | Runnable examples + playground |
| **Go deep** | Can I handle follow-ups? | In-depth sections + edge cases |
| **Prove it** | Would I pass the interview? | Q&A + challenges + mastery gate |

Notice what's missing: **"memorize."** Understanding first, not flashcard cramming alone (though spaced repetition supports retention).

---

## Target learner

- Mid-level developers preparing for senior/staff interviews
- Bootcamp grads filling conceptual gaps
- Engineers switching stacks (e.g. backend → full-stack React)
- Anyone tired of "I read the answer but don't really get it"
